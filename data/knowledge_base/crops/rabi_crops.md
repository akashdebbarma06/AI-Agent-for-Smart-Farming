# Rabi Season Crop Guide

> **Source:** KrishiMitra AI Knowledge Base — Agricultural Reference Document

## What is the Rabi Season?

The Rabi season runs from **October/November to March/April** in India. Crops are sown after the monsoon rains retreat and are harvested before summer heat intensifies. Rabi crops require cool temperatures during growth and moderate water availability (mostly irrigation-dependent).

---

## Major Rabi Crops

### 1. Wheat
- **Suitable Soil:** Well-drained loamy or clayey-loam; pH 6.0–7.5
- **Sowing Time:** October–November (timely sown); November (late sown)
- **Harvest Time:** March–April
- **Water Requirement:** 4–5 irrigations; critical stages — crown root initiation (21 days), tillering, jointing, flowering, grain filling
- **Key States:** Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan
- **Notes:** Timely sowing (October 25–November 15) gives maximum yield. Late sowing reduces yield by 1–1.5% per day after November 25.

### 2. Mustard (Rapeseed)
- **Suitable Soil:** Sandy loam to loam; pH 6.5–7.5; tolerates slight salinity
- **Sowing Time:** October–November
- **Harvest Time:** February–March
- **Water Requirement:** 2–3 irrigations; drought-tolerant once established
- **Key States:** Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh, West Bengal
- **Notes:** Do not irrigate at flowering stage (causes shattering). Apply sulphur fertiliser at 20–30 kg/ha for better oil content.

### 3. Chickpea (Gram / Chana)
- **Suitable Soil:** Well-drained loamy or sandy-loam; pH 6.0–8.0; tolerates low fertility
- **Sowing Time:** October–November
- **Harvest Time:** February–March
- **Water Requirement:** 1–2 irrigations only; excess moisture causes disease
- **Key States:** Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh
- **Notes:** Chickpea is a legume — it fixes atmospheric nitrogen. Do not apply excess nitrogen fertiliser. Pre-sowing seed inoculation with *Mesorhizobium ciceri* is recommended.

### 4. Lentil (Masoor)
- **Suitable Soil:** Loamy to clay-loam; pH 6.0–8.0
- **Sowing Time:** October–November
- **Harvest Time:** March
- **Water Requirement:** 1–2 light irrigations; rain-fed cultivation possible in medium-rainfall areas
- **Key States:** Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal
- **Notes:** Lentil is susceptible to rust disease in humid conditions. Avoid overhead irrigation during pod formation.

### 5. Potato
- **Suitable Soil:** Sandy loam or loam with good drainage; pH 5.5–6.5; rich in organic matter
- **Sowing Time:** October–November (plains); February–March (hills)
- **Harvest Time:** January–February (plains)
- **Water Requirement:** 4–5 irrigations; critical at tuber initiation and bulking stage
- **Key States:** Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Bihar, Punjab, Gujarat
- **Notes:** Potato is susceptible to late blight (*Phytophthora infestans*). Monitor closely during cool, humid weather. Early harvest if late blight pressure is high.

### 6. Barley
- **Suitable Soil:** Light loamy to sandy-loam; tolerates saline-alkaline conditions better than wheat
- **Sowing Time:** October–November
- **Harvest Time:** March–April
- **Water Requirement:** 2–3 irrigations; more drought-tolerant than wheat
- **Key States:** Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Himachal Pradesh, Uttarakhand
- **Notes:** Barley is suitable for salt-affected and low-fertility soils where wheat fails.
