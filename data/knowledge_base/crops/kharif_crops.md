## What is the Kharif Season?

The Kharif season runs from **June to October** in India, coinciding with the southwest monsoon. Crops are sown at the beginning of the rains and harvested in autumn. Kharif crops require warm temperatures, moderate to heavy rainfall, and long daylight hours.

---

## Major Kharif Crops

### 1. Paddy (Rice)
- **Suitable Soil:** Clayey or loamy soil that retains water well; alluvial soil is ideal
- **Rainfall Requirement:** 100–200 cm per season
- **Sowing Time:** June–July (transplanting after 25–30 days of nursery)
- **Harvest Time:** October–November
- **Key States:** West Bengal, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu
- **Notes:** Paddy requires standing water (puddled fields) during the vegetative stage. Avoid waterlogging during flowering.

### 2. Maize (Corn)
- **Suitable Soil:** Well-drained loamy or sandy-loam soil; pH 6.0–7.5
- **Rainfall Requirement:** 60–90 cm; supplemental irrigation needed in dry spells
- **Sowing Time:** June–July
- **Harvest Time:** September–October
- **Key States:** Karnataka, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh
- **Notes:** Maize does not tolerate waterlogging. Apply a nitrogenous fertiliser top-dressing 30 days after sowing.

### 3. Cotton
- **Suitable Soil:** Black cotton soil (Vertisol); deep, well-drained
- **Rainfall Requirement:** 50–100 cm; drought-tolerant once established
- **Sowing Time:** May–June (early sowing improves yield)
- **Harvest Time:** November–January (picking done in stages)
- **Key States:** Maharashtra, Gujarat, Telangana, Punjab, Haryana
- **Notes:** Cotton is highly sensitive to boll weevil and bollworm. Scout fields weekly during boll formation.

### 4. Soybean
- **Suitable Soil:** Well-drained clayey-loam; pH 6.0–7.0
- **Rainfall Requirement:** 60–80 cm; sensitive to drought at flowering
- **Sowing Time:** June–July (after first good rains)
- **Harvest Time:** October
- **Key States:** Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Rajasthan
- **Notes:** Inoculate seeds with *Rhizobium japonicum* to enhance nitrogen fixation. Avoid over-irrigation.

### 5. Groundnut (Peanut)
- **Suitable Soil:** Sandy loam or light soils with good drainage; pH 6.0–6.5
- **Rainfall Requirement:** 50–75 cm
- **Sowing Time:** June–July
- **Harvest Time:** September–October
- **Key States:** Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Rajasthan
- **Notes:** Calcium is critical during pegging stage. Apply gypsum at 200 kg/ha before first rain after sowing.

### 6. Bajra (Pearl Millet)
- **Suitable Soil:** Sandy to loamy; highly drought-tolerant; pH 6.5–8.0
- **Rainfall Requirement:** 40–60 cm; grows well in low-rainfall regions
- **Sowing Time:** June–July
- **Harvest Time:** September–October
- **Key States:** Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh
- **Notes:** Bajra is the most drought-resistant grain crop suitable for arid and semi-arid zones.
