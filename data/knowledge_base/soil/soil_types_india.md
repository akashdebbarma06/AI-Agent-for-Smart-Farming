# Soil Types of India — Agricultural Guide

> **Source:** KrishiMitra AI Knowledge Base — Agricultural Reference Document  
> **Domain:** Soil Information

---

## Introduction

India has eight major soil types. Understanding your soil type is the first step to selecting the right crop and fertiliser. Each soil type has distinct characteristics in terms of water retention, drainage, nutrient holding capacity, and pH.

---

## 1. Alluvial Soil

- **Where Found:** Indo-Gangetic Plains (Punjab, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Assam river valleys)
- **Colour:** Light grey to brown
- **Texture:** Sandy loam to loam; varies from coarse (khadar) to fine (bhangar)
- **pH:** 6.5–8.0 (neutral to slightly alkaline)
- **Fertility:** High in potash and lime; deficient in nitrogen and humus
- **Water Retention:** Moderate to good
- **Best Crops:** Wheat, rice, sugarcane, maize, oilseeds, pulses, vegetables
- **Key Tip:** Apply organic matter (compost/FYM) regularly to replenish nitrogen and humus.

---

## 2. Black Cotton Soil (Regur / Vertisol)

- **Where Found:** Deccan Plateau — Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka
- **Colour:** Black (due to titaniferous magnetite and humus)
- **Texture:** Heavy clay; shrinks and cracks in dry weather
- **pH:** 7.2–8.5 (neutral to alkaline)
- **Fertility:** Rich in calcium, potassium, magnesium; deficient in nitrogen and phosphorus
- **Water Retention:** Very high (self-mulching)
- **Best Crops:** Cotton (primary), soybean, jowar, sunflower, wheat (with irrigation)
- **Key Tip:** Poor drainage; do not sow in waterlogged conditions. Deep ploughing every 3 years improves structure.

---

## 3. Red Soil

- **Where Found:** Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh (southern parts), Odisha, Chhattisgarh, parts of Karnataka
- **Colour:** Red (due to iron oxide); turns yellow when hydrated
- **Texture:** Sandy loam to loam; coarse-grained; well-drained
- **pH:** 6.0–7.5 (slightly acidic to neutral)
- **Fertility:** Low in nitrogen, phosphorus, and humus; adequate potash
- **Water Retention:** Poor (dries out quickly)
- **Best Crops:** Groundnut, millets (bajra, jowar), cotton, tobacco, pulses
- **Key Tip:** Lime application improves fertility of highly acidic red soils. Irrigation essential for most crops.

---

## 4. Laterite Soil

- **Where Found:** Kerala, Karnataka (western ghats), Goa, Jharkhand, Odisha, Northeast India
- **Colour:** Red-brown to yellow-brown; brick-like when dry
- **Texture:** Coarse, porous, well-drained; hardened in sunlight
- **pH:** 4.5–6.0 (acidic)
- **Fertility:** Heavily leached; low in nitrogen, phosphorus, calcium, magnesium
- **Water Retention:** Poor
- **Best Crops:** Tea, coffee, cashew, rubber, coconut, areca nut
- **Key Tip:** Requires heavy organic matter input and liming to grow food crops. Mulching is critical to prevent further leaching.

---

## 5. Sandy / Desert Soil

- **Where Found:** Rajasthan, parts of Punjab and Haryana
- **Colour:** Light brown to yellow
- **Texture:** Coarse sand; very low clay content
- **pH:** 7.0–8.5 (neutral to alkaline)
- **Fertility:** Very low; poor in all nutrients
- **Water Retention:** Very poor (water drains quickly)
- **Best Crops:** Bajra (pearl millet), cluster bean (guar), moth bean, barley, drought-tolerant varieties
- **Key Tip:** Drip irrigation is highly efficient for this soil type. Windbreaks (shelter belts) reduce erosion.

---

## 6. Forest / Hill Soil

- **Where Found:** Hilly and mountainous regions — Himalayan foothills, Northeast India, Western and Eastern Ghats
- **Colour:** Dark brown to grey
- **Texture:** Variable; loamy to sandy-loam with high organic content in upper layers
- **pH:** 4.0–6.0 (acidic)
- **Best Crops:** Tea, spices (cardamom, ginger), apple, temperate vegetables, maize
- **Key Tip:** Terracing is essential on slopes to prevent erosion. Acidic soils may need liming.

## Soil Type vs. Crop Suitability Summary

| Soil Type | Primary Crops | Common Deficiency |
|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| Alluvial | Rice, Wheat, Sugarcane | N, Organic matter |
| Black Cotton | Cotton, Soybean, Jowar | N, P |
| Red | Groundnut, Millets, Pulses | N, P, Humus |
| Laterite | Tea, Coffee, Cashew | All nutrients |
| Sandy/Desert | Bajra, Guar, Barley | All nutrients |
| Forest/Hill | Tea, Spices, Maize | Lime needed |

---

## Advisory Note

Soil type alone does not determine crop success. Local variety selection, irrigation availability, and microclimate all play important roles. Get a professional soil test before investing in expensive inputs.
